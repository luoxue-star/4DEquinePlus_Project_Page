<HighlightedSection color="#ffffff">
  <h2>Abstract</h2>
  <p style="font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; font-size: 1.2rem; line-height: 1.3;">4D reconstruction of equines from monocular videos is important for quantitative behavior analysis and the creation of realistic digital avatars. However, existing methods often jointly optimize motion and appearance over an entire video, making reconstruction computationally expensive and dependent on sufficient viewpoint coverage. To address these limitations, we introduce 4DEquine+, an extended version of our 4DEquine framework. 4DEquine+ disentangles the problem into dynamic motion recovery and static appearance reconstruction. Specifically, AniMoFormer combines a spatio-temporal transformer with a post-optimization stage to estimate smooth, image-aligned pose and shape sequences, while EquineGS reconstructs a high-fidelity, animatable 3D Gaussian avatar in a feed-forward manner. To enable effective training without real 4D annotations, we construct three complementary synthetic datasets: VarenPoser provides annotated motion sequences, VarenTex supplies appearance-consistent multi-view images, and the newly introduced VarenWan contains generative 180° turn-around videos with diverse identities of horse, zebra and donkey, featuring realistic manes and tails. Compared with 4DEquine, EquineGS in 4DEquine+ conditions on multiple frames sampled from the whole video, exploiting complementary observations to improve reconstruction under occlusion and truncation. We further introduce Subject-Centric Token Pruning (SCTP), which discards redundant background tokens to reduce the additional aggregation cost of multi-image conditioning. Trained entirely on synthetic data, 4DEquine+ achieves state-of-the-art motion recovery performance on APT36K and AiM datasets, and yields the best appearance reconstruction results on AiM-Horse, AiM-Zebra, and our in-the-wild EquineVideo benchmark. With four conditioning images, 4DEquine+ reduces LPIPS over 4DEquine by 20.3%–27.2%. SCTP provides speedups of 1.16× and 1.39× for four and sixteen conditioning images, respectively, with negligible degradation in reconstruction quality.</p>
</HighlightedSection>

<HighlightedSection color="#ffffff">
  <h2>4DEquine+</h2>
  <p style="font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; font-size: 1.2rem; line-height: 1.3;">The 4DEquine+ framework retains two disentangled branches for dynamic motion recovery and static appearance reconstruction. In AniMoFormer, a Spatial Transformer extracts per-frame features, while a Temporal Transformer models motion across each video window. A VAREN Transformer Decoder predicts pose, shape, and camera parameters, followed by Post-Optimization using 2D keypoints and silhouettes to produce accurate, temporally smooth, image-aligned posed meshes; sliding-window inference supports videos of arbitrary length. In EquineGS, one or more frames uniformly sampled from the same monocular video are independently encoded by a shared DINOv3 backbone. Subject-Centric Token Pruning (SCTP) retains foreground and boundary tokens while discarding redundant background tokens. Five Dual-Stream Transformer Gaussian (DSTG) blocks then fuse the pruned image features with point features from canonical upsampled VAREN vertices to predict a canonical 3D Gaussian avatar. Finally, VAREN-driven Linear Blend Skinning (LBS) animates the avatar with the recovered motion to produce the complete 4D reconstruction. Both branches are trained entirely on synthetic data: AniMoFormer uses VarenPoser, while EquineGS uses VarenTex and VarenWan. For more details, please refer to our paper.</p>
